## The challenge

Explore how webcam hand tracking can control a visually expressive desktop application without a browser or a web server.

## My contribution

- Built hand tracking, gesture interpretation and a native desktop interface.
- Connected fire, lightning and plasma effects to continuous gesture states.
- Added smoothing and short tracking-loss grace periods to keep interaction stable.

## Engineering decisions

- OpenCV captures frames; MediaPipe provides hand landmarks; PySide6 hosts the interface; OpenGL renders the effects.
- Timestamped snapshots connect capture, tracking, simulation and rendering without blocking one another.
- The fire effect combines a density/flow atlas with procedural shading. Other effects use procedural rendering.

## Outcome

An experimental local Windows application. It requires a webcam and OpenGL 3.3. It is not a production client delivery or a web demo.
