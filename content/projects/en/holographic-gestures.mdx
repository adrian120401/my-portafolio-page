## The challenge

Make 3D manipulation feel direct through a webcam, while keeping movement, rotation, scaling and model selection from competing with each other.

## My contribution

- Built hand gestures for moving, rotating and scaling holograms, with particle-based disintegration and reconstruction.
- Added an inspection mode that separates a vehicle into 12 assemblies, selects visible parts by depth and lets the user inspect and reassemble them.
- Kept the camera's natural image visible and provided keyboard controls alongside hand tracking.

## Engineering decisions

- Separated capture, tracking, gesture interpretation and scene state from rendering.
- Used confirmation times, hysteresis and recalibrated anchors to avoid conflicting gestures and jumps after tracking loss.
- Used ModernGL and GLSL for the holographic materials and GPU particles; inspection selection uses an ID framebuffer with depth.

## Outcome

A local desktop prototype combining computer vision and 3D graphics. The recorded demo shows the application; the portfolio player does not perform live webcam tracking. Setup, gestures and implementation details are available in the public repository.
