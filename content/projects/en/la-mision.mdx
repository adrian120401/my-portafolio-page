## The challenge

Support different moments in a race participant’s journey: exploring courses, registering and accessing runner information. The deliveries address separate workflows for the same client.

## My contribution

- Built a registration system with user accounts, validation and password recovery.
- Integrated PayPal, Mercado Pago and automated email notifications.
- Developed race tracking and information views with interactive maps and elevation profiles.
- The current public race website is included alongside registration and tracking in this case.

## Engineering decisions

- Registration and tracking use React, Node.js, Express and MySQL, according to the original delivery documentation.
- React Leaflet powers interactive course maps. Payment and email integrations support the registration workflow.
- The public website and operational applications are separate deliveries; the stack listed here describes the registration and tracking systems.

## Outcome

Several related deliveries are presented together to explain the client relationship without counting the same product as unrelated projects. The current race website links to course and registration information.
