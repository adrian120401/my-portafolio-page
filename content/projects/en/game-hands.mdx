## The challenge

Connect computer vision to game controls while keeping tracking logic independent of gameplay.

## My contribution

- Built movement, firing, shielding and a charged special action from hand gestures.
- Separated gesture events from the game world and combined them with keyboard fallback.
- Implemented smoothing, hysteresis and dwell to avoid unstable inputs.

## Engineering decisions

- The gesture controller emits snapshots and events without depending on the game.
- OpenGL renders the 3D world; a native PySide6 shell hosts it.
- The camera and asynchronous tracker exchange recent frames through a mailbox.

## Outcome

A local interaction showcase for Windows with webcam or keyboard input. It explores a control layer that can be reused beyond a single game.
