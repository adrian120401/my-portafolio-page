## The challenge

Build an ERP and inventory platform around the operational workflows of businesses in Argentina. The engineering challenge connects domain behavior, stock management and a usable frontend.

## My contribution

- Defined architecture and development priorities for the platform.
- Coordinated and mentored developers across backend and frontend delivery.
- Supported improvements to maintainability, scalability and performance in inventory workflows.

## Engineering decisions

- Java supports the business backend; React provides the web application; AWS supports the infrastructure.
- Architecture and implementation priorities were aligned with the ERP and inventory domain.
- My contribution includes technical direction and team coordination, not a claim that I built the entire product alone.

## Outcome

The platform is used by businesses in Argentina. The public website is linked above; its screenshot is a view of the website, not a private customer dashboard.
